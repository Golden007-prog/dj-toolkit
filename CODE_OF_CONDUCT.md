# Code of conduct

DJ Toolkit follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions and the app's own Q&A) agrees to it. In short:

- **Be kind and patient.** People here range from first-time contributors to senior engineers, and from bedroom DJs
  to touring ones.
- **Critique ideas, not people.** "I'd do this differently, because…" is welcome everywhere.
- **No harassment**, insults, discrimination, or sexualised language or imagery, in any project space.
- **Assume good faith**, and ask when you're unsure what someone meant.

## Reporting

Tell the maintainers privately, by email: the address is on the maintainers' GitHub profiles. If your report is
about a maintainer, write to another one. Reports are kept confidential, and you'll get a reply within a week.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and warn or ban people, following the Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
